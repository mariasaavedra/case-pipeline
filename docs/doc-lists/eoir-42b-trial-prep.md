# Document List for Trial Preparation – EOIR-42B (Cancellation of Removal)

## English

Your Individual Hearing (trial) is scheduled. We must update your application and supporting documents. Review the application copies we gave you. List any changes and gather updated documents. Your paralegal will need these after you pay the Trial Preparation Fee.

### Evidence updates

**Continuous Physical Presence**

- [ ] Utility Bills
- [ ] Lease Agreements / Mortgage Statements
- [ ] Bank Statements
- [ ] School Records
- [ ] Insurance Records
- [ ] Other records showing your name and date

**Qualifying Relative**

- [ ] Birth Certificates
- [ ] Permanent Resident Cards
- [ ] Marriage Certificate

**Hardship**

- [ ] Medical / Mental Health Records
- [ ] Therapist or Counseling Records
- [ ] Special Education Documents
- [ ] Financial Hardship Evidence
- [ ] Caregiving Responsibilities
- [ ] Affidavits from Family Members
- [ ] Teacher / Doctor / Religious Leader Letters

\*\* Letters and Affidavits MUST be signed, dated and include contact information.

**Good Moral Character**

- [ ] Letters of Support
- [ ] Church Records
- [ ] Volunteer Records / Community Service Records
- [ ] Certificates / Awards or Recognition
- [ ] Educational Records / Professional Licenses
- [ ] Evidence of Financial Support Provided to Family

\*\* Letters of Support MUST be signed, dated and include contact information.

**Criminal History (if applicable)**

- [ ] Certified Court Dispositions
- [ ] Charging Documents
- [ ] Rehabilitation Records

**Employment & Tax Documents**

- [ ] Most Recent Tax Return Transcripts
- [ ] Recent Pay Stubs / Employment Verification Letter
- [ ] Self-Employment / Business Records
- [ ] W-2s / 1099s

Su audiencia individual (juicio) está programada. Debemos actualizar su aplicación y los documentos de respaldo. Revise las copias de cliente que le dimos. Anote los cambios y reúna los documentos actualizados. Su asistente legal los necesitará después de que usted pague los honorarios de preparación para el juicio.
