Alerts are grouped by severity (critical, warning, info). Filter by attorney or severity. Groups include:

- **Overdue deadlines** and **stale cases** (no update in 30+ days).
- **Appeal & Federal Deadlines:** the 30-day BIA appeal clock after a removal order, appeal briefs due, litigation due within 14 days.
- **I-918B Expiring** and **I-918B Requests Pending**.
- **Mail to review:** notices that need a person to match them.
- **Assigned to inactive staff:** work held only by people who left.
