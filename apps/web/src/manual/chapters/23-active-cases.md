**Active Cases** lists every open case once, sorted by urgency (overdue, due soon, later, no date).

- The **summary table** has one row per paralegal. Click a number or a name to filter the list.
- Chips filter by person, urgency, **Only court**, or **North Pole** (parked cases).
- Click a case to expand it and change its status, dates or other fields in place. Changes go to Monday.
