## Log a call

1. Press **+ Log call** in the top bar (any page).
2. Type the caller's number or name. Pick the client when they come up — their recent notes show, and **View profile** opens their case.
3. Pick the **Status** and **Language**. **More fields** has *Taken by* and *Highlight for*.
4. Write what the call was about.
5. Optional: tick **Send a text** to text the caller after saving (see [Texting clients](/manual/texting)).
6. Optional: tick **this call is a jail intake** to start a jail intake (see [Jail intakes](/manual/jail-intakes)).
7. Press **Save** (or **Save + send text**).

## The Call Log page

**Call Log** in the sidebar lists every call. Filter by status, who took it, day, or **unlinked only** (calls not tied to a client yet).

- Click the caller's name or the pencil to **edit** a call: every field, plus its Monday notes thread and **Add note**.
- 💬 opens the call straight on its notes.
- Click the client to open their case.
- **Status** and **Highlighted For** change right in the table.
