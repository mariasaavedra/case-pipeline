**Receptionists** in the sidebar lists the consults on every attorney's board, today by default. Pick **Today**, **Tomorrow** or **Next 7 days**, an attorney, or **Not prepped** to see what's left.

## Prep a consult

1. Find the consult and press **Prep**.
2. Fill in **Type of appt** (Zoom, phone, in person, **Detained appt** — then pick the detention center), **Needs interpreter?** and **How to proceed**.
3. Check **Client wrote (Calendly)**. You can correct it; your correction replaces the client's part of the description.
4. Under **Documents**, pick the client's files the attorney should see. **Browse SharePoint…** lets you upload new ones.
5. Optional: **Has DMS?** adds their DMS link.
6. Press **Save prep note**.

Saving posts the prep as a note on the client's profile, pins it on the appointment, and adds a "Consult Prep Note" entry. The attorney sees it on their [Appointments](/manual/appointments) page within a minute.

> The phone number or description you edit in the prep is saved back to the profile and the appointment.

## "No profile" consults

A Calendly booking sometimes arrives without a client profile linked.

1. Press **Link client** on the row.
2. Search for the client (the name from the appointment is already filled in).
3. Press **Link**.

To change an existing link, do it in Monday.

## Book a consult

1. Press **+ Book Appt** at the top.
2. Search for the client.
3. Pick the attorney, the date and a 30-minute slot.
4. Save.

## Move a consult

Press **Edit** on the row to change the attorney, date or time. A new attorney moves the appointment to their board in Monday.

> Calendly is **not** changed. Update the booking in Calendly too.

## Tell the attorney the client is ready (Slack)

Right after **Save prep note**, the popup shows the attorney's Slack message, drafted from your prep ("LB's 1:00 PM Ready", with interpreter and Zoom link — never a phone number).

1. Check the message and edit it if needed.
2. Press **Queue message** to post it at the consult's time, or **Skip**.
3. The **Slack Queue** tab at the top of the page lists what's waiting. Edit or delete a message until it's sent; **Send now** posts it at once, **Retry now** re-sends a failed one.

A message more than 60 minutes late is marked failed instead of posted.

## Focus view

**Focus** opens the client's notes and documents in a popup, for a quick look during a call.
