**Prescheduling** lists the **Paid Fee Ks**: hired clients we're collecting documents from before the work starts.

- The **summary table** has one row per paralegal and one column per **PS Stage**. The colour is the longest wait since the hire (30 and 60 days). Click a number to see those cases.
- **Not cooperating** marks a client whose reminder went out 14+ days ago with no evidence since.
- **North Pole** cases are parked. They're hidden unless you turn them on.

To change a case's PS Stage or dates, use [My Cases](/manual/my-cases), or Monday.
