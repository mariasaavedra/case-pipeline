## For everyone

- **My account:** your profile, which name on the boards is you (**I am…**), and your **Monday.com connection**. Amber here means reconnect — one click.
- **Preferences:** theme, date format, language, start page, sidebar, and **Show screen codes**.

## For admins

- **Firm setup:** attorney appointment boards, status tags, urgency thresholds, document settings (firm address, attorneys, detention facilities), text templates, **Fee K Types** (incl. **Documents…**: what a client's Buzón asks them to send for that case type, in English and Spanish; **Use the suggested list** fills one in to review), **Buzón texts** (the link texted when a case is paid, reminders on/off and their days and hours, the "we received" text), **Consult document lists**.
- **Integrations:** Slack notifications (on/off and channel per event, test message) and RingCentral texting (check the connection).
- **Workflows:** everything the app does on its own, whether it's on, and when it last ran.
- **Staff:** the staff roster — everyone, their roles and every spelling of their name on the boards — and **Offboarding**.
- **Admin:** users (role, staff record, disable), sync health, Monday API usage and the audit log.
