## New intake

1. **Jail Intakes → + New intake** (or tick *this call is a jail intake* when logging a call).
2. Fill in the detainee, facility and point of contact.
3. Pick the status: **New Detainee** or **Payment link sent. Waiting on payment**.
4. Save.

## Book the consult on a paid intake

When an intake is **Needs to be scheduled**:

1. Click the intake to open it.
2. Press **Book consult**, pick the attorney, date and time.
3. Save. The consult date and attorney are written to Monday.
4. Open the item in Monday (the link is shown) and press **Create Appt** there.

Monday's own automations then make the appointment and the profile. The app can't press Monday buttons for you.

> The intake's point of contact is copied to the Fee K's "POC Full Name" automatically when the Fee K is made.
