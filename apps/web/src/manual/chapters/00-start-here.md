The app is the firm's dashboard on top of **Monday.com**. Everything you see comes from the Monday boards (Profiles, Fee Ks, Open Forms, Court Cases, appointments…), and most changes you make here are written straight back to Monday under your name.

> **New to the app?** Read this chapter, then the chapter for your job: [Reception](/manual/reception), [Appointments](/manual/appointments) for attorneys, [My Cases](/manual/my-cases) for paralegals, [Contracts](/manual/contracts) for billing.

## Sign in

1. Open the app and press **Sign in with Microsoft**.
2. Use your firm Microsoft 365 account (the same one as Outlook).
3. The first time, a popup asks you to **connect your Monday.com account**. Press **Connect** and allow it.

Connecting Monday puts **your name** on the notes and changes you make. If you skip it (**Not now**), your changes still go through under the firm's shared account, and the popup comes back in 12 hours.

## Finding your way around

- **Sidebar (left):** one link per page. **Manual** and **Settings** are at the bottom.
- **Top bar:** **? Help** opens the manual chapter for the page you're on. **+ Log call** opens the call form from any page.
- **Client names** are links almost everywhere. Clicking one opens the client's case in a popup over the page you're on, so you don't lose your place. Press **Back** or **×** to close it.

### Make the sidebar yours

1. Click **Customize** at the bottom of the sidebar.
2. Drag pages by the dotted handle to reorder them. Click the eye to hide one you don't use.
3. **+ New folder** groups pages. Drag pages into it.
4. Click **Done**. Your layout follows you to any computer; it doesn't change anyone else's. **Reset** puts everything back.

## Screen codes

Every page, popup and menu shows a small grey code, like **P14** (a page), **M21** (a popup) or **D6** (a dropdown). Put the code in a request — *"M21 Payment links: add a note field"* — so everyone knows exactly which screen you mean.

Don't want to see them? **Settings → Preferences → Show screen codes**.

## How fresh is the data?

- A change made **in Monday** shows up here within about **a minute**.
- A change made **here** shows at once, and reaches Monday within seconds.
- If Monday is down, your change is **queued** and sent automatically when Monday is back. You don't need to redo it.

> **Changed since you opened it?** If someone changed the same field in Monday after you opened the page, the app refuses your change and asks you to reload. That stops two people overwriting each other.

## Home page

Home shows six number cards (open forms, pending contracts, paid Fee Ks, deadlines, hearings, alerts) and your watchlist and recently viewed clients. Click a card to see every case behind the number.

The version badge on Home opens **What's new**: every change to the app, newest first. It's also in this manual: [What's new](/manual/whats-new).
