## I changed something and it changed back

Someone changed the same thing in Monday after you opened the page. Reload the page and make your change again.

## My notes say they're from the firm, not me

Your Monday connection is missing or out of date. **Settings → My account → Monday.com → Connect** (or Reconnect).

## A client I linked in Monday still shows "No profile"

Links made in Monday can take until the next night's full refresh to show. Use **Link client** on the Reception page instead — it shows at once.

## Monday is down

Keep working. Changes are queued and sent when Monday is back.

## The page looks out of date

Reload the page (⌘R / Ctrl+R). If it still looks wrong, tell the admin with the **screen code** from the bottom-right corner.

## Asking for a change

Write the screen code and what you want: *"P14 Contracts: show the client's phone on each row."* The codes are explained in [Start here](/manual/start-here#screen-codes).
