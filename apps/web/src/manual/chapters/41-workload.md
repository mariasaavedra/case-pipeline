One row per person with their open items on Active Cases, Prescheduling, Contracts, Court Cases and FOIAs. Two lists at the bottom matter most: **No one assigned** and **Held only by inactive staff**.

When someone leaves, use **Settings → Staff → Offboard…** to hand their open work to others.
