Texts go out from the firm's main number through RingCentral. They're never sent automatically — always by you, one at a time.

## Send a text

From a client's page (**Text** next to the phone), from **+ Log call** (tick **Send a text**), or from **Payment links**:

1. Check the number. **Use Phone 2 instead** switches numbers.
2. Pick **English** or **Español** (it starts on the client's language).
3. Pick a template. The client's first name and any link are filled in.
4. Edit the text if needed.
5. Press **Send text**.

A sent text is logged on the client's profile as a "Text message" entry.

## The app refuses to send when

- The number isn't a US number.
- A `{{placeholder}}` is still unfilled.
- The same text went to the same number in the last 2 minutes.

## Templates

Anyone can **Edit template** or add **+ New template** from the text popup; everyone sees the change. Admins reorder and delete them in Settings → Firm → Text templates.

> If the button says **Send (test)**, texting is in test mode and nothing is sent.
