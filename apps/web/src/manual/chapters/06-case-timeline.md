Open a client, then the **Timeline** tab. It's also a tab in the client popup and on Appointments.

## Read the top

- **Where it stands:** the case in one or two lines.
- **Needs attention:** things to fix — a hearing with no outcome, a missed deadline, a motion still waiting on the judge. A hearing with **HEARING NOTES** logged the same day counts as attended, and the notes show on the hearing.
- **Coming up:** the next hearings, deadlines and appointments.

## The chart

One lane each for **court**, **USCIS**, **fees**, **meetings** and **notes per month**. Pick 12 months, 18 months, 3 years or everything. Click a dot to jump to it in the list below.

- A **crossed-out** event is a hearing, interview or consult that was moved. The old date stays, with the new date and who moved it.
- Thin ticks are **status changes**. Untick **Status changes** to hide them.

## Fix something from the timeline

Click any event, a list row or a **Coming up** item. A panel opens with that Monday item's fields:

1. Change a field. It saves when you click away.
2. A hearing with no outcome gets one-click outcomes: **Attended**, **Rescheduled / not happening**, **Withdrew**, **IJ no-show**.
3. Write a note about the event at the bottom.
4. **History** shows every change made to the item in Monday, newest first, and who made it.

The **Record outcome**, **Fix** and **Record decision** buttons under *Needs attention* open the same panel on the right item.
