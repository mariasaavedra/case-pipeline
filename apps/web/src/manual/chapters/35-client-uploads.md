Everything clients sent through their **Buzón** (their private upload link), newest first. By default you only see what nobody has looked at yet; **Show all** shows everything.

Each row has:

- the client (opens their **Documents** tab) and the document it was for (or what the client said it was);
- the file, linked to its copy in SharePoint once it's filed;
- the client's note, if they wrote one;
- where it stands: filed, being filed, or waiting (for example, no E-File on the profile yet).

To review files:

1. Open the file.
2. If it's fine, press **Reviewed**. **Undo** puts it back on the list.

## What happens on its own

- **Several photos of one document** (front and back of an ID, the pages of a birth certificate) go to SharePoint as **one PDF**.
- **iPhone photos** (HEIC) go to SharePoint as **JPG**, so every PC opens them.
- **A letter from USCIS or the court** is also read like scanned mail and shows in **Alerts → Mail to review**, tied to the client. The row says **→ Mail to review**.

Moving or renaming a file is still done in SharePoint.
