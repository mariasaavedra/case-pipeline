The target date is the firm's internal goal for an Open Form. Only an admin sets or changes it. Paralegals see it on their case card, and an attorney grants an extension when they need one.

**Target Dates** lists every open Open Form in five groups: **Overdue**, **Due in 7 days**, **Due in 8–14 days**, **Later** and **No target date**. The counts at the top jump to each group. Click a paralegal's name to see only their cases.

On each row:

- **Moved 2×**: how many times the date has moved to another day.
- **Changed by …** (red): someone who isn't an admin changed the date in Monday. Check with them, and move it back if needed.

## Move a date (extension)

1. Click **Move date** on the row.
2. Pick the new date, or use **+7 / +14 / +30 days**.
3. **Extension granted by**: the attorney who granted it (filled in with the case's attorney).
4. Optional: a **Reason**.
5. Click **Move date**.

The date changes in Monday, and an update on the Open Form says when it moved, who granted it and why, so the paralegal sees it there.

For a case with no date, **Set date** works the same way, without the extension part.

## Slack digest

On weekdays at 8 AM, each admin gets a Slack direct message listing the target dates that are overdue or due within 7 days, grouped by paralegal, with a link to this page. To receive it, your Slack id must be on your entry in **Settings → Staff**. Turn it on or off in **Settings → Integrations → Target dates digest**.
