**Court Cases** has three tabs.

## Docket

Upcoming hearings by week (30 / 60 / 90 days or all). Filter by judge, attorney, paralegal or hearing type. Readiness colours: a Trial needs prep 60–90 days ahead, a Master hearing 14 days.

## Prep Pipeline

Cases grouped by **Case Prep Status**. Move a case to another stage with the stage picker; it asks *Move to X?* and writes to Monday.

## Motions

Open motions: hearing within 14 days with a motion still open, to send, waiting for the judge (60 / 90-day aging), and decided in the last 30 days. Change a motion's status from its picker. **Filed**, **Granted** and **Denied** ask for the date.

## Needs cleanup on Monday

Chips at the top count cases to fix in Monday: hearing date passed, awaiting new date, no date, profile not connected.
