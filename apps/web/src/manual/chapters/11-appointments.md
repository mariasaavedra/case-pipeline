**Appointments** opens on **your own** calendar for **today**. The tabs at the top show another attorney's day; **‹ Today ›** and the date picker change the day. The page refreshes itself every minute.

> **Opens on the wrong calendar?** An admin links you to your board in Settings → Staff (your staff record's attorney board).

## The day

One row per appointment, time first. Each shows **Prepped** / **Not prepped**, and the next one is marked **Next**. One that already started without an outcome says **Needs outcome**. A detainee consult has a red **Detained** tag.

## Before the consult

Click a client. You'll see:

- Zoom / phone / interpreter / detained, as reception prepped it.
- **Consult prep:** who prepped and when, reception's description, what the client wrote, and the documents reception picked.
- **Notes:** the client's notes. **By attorneys** shows only attorneys' notes.
- **Timeline:** the [case timeline](/manual/case-timeline).
- **E-file / Consult file:** the client's SharePoint folders.
- **Open full file** opens the client page.

## After the consult

1. Set the outcome: a **Quick outcome** button (Hire, No Hire, No Hire for Now, Hold for Docs, No Action Needed, Send G-review link), or click **Status** for every status. Detainee consults get **Det Hire / Det No Hire**.
2. Write your **Consult note**.
3. On a hire, fill **Fees for the contract** (case type, AF, FF, PF). It opens by itself on Hire.
4. Press **Save consult note**.

Saving does three things:

- Adds a "Consult note" to the client's profile (with a `Contract: …` line when you entered fees).
- Writes the **Consultation Summary** with your note into the client's **CONSULT** folder in SharePoint.
- Keeps the fees, so the Fee K staff create in Monday gets its empty case type and fees filled in.

**+ New contract** opens the contract form with the client already picked. See [Contracts](/manual/contracts).
