## File a letter

1. **Mail → choose (or drop) the PDF of one letter.**
2. The app reads it: notice type, receipt number, dates, names, A-number. **Edit** fixes anything misread.
3. Check the **client** it matched, or search for the right one. Optionally pick the **Open Form** it belongs to.
4. Check the file name. It goes to the client's **E-File → CC**.
5. Optional: **E-mail the client** — English and Spanish, from your Outlook, with the letter attached. Preview it first.
6. Add a note for the profile.
7. Press **File it**.

If a step fails, press **File it** again; it carries on from where it stopped. **Filed just now** lists what you've filed this session.

## Mail to review (Alerts)

Notices that couldn't be matched to exactly one case show in [Alerts](/manual/alerts) under *Mail to review*. Open one, then assign it to the right Open Form or dismiss it with a note.
